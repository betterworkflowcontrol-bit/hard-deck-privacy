Hard Deck — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Hard Deck is a skydiving logbook app. This page explains, in plain English, what information the app uses, why, and how you can delete it.

Developer: Mark  ·  Contact: betterworkflowcontrol@gmail.com

The short version

  • Hard Deck is a logbook. Its main job is to record your jumps, and it does that using your phone's sensors and location while you're checked in.
  • By default, everything stays on your phone. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you turn on cloud sync or sign in.
  • If you sign in with Apple to back up your logbook or connect with friends, a small amount of information is stored securely so that works.
  • You can delete your account and everything tied to it, inside the app, at any time.
  • We don't show ads, don't sell your data, and don't track you across other apps or websites.

If you never turn on sync, never sign in, and never use dictation, nothing listed above leaves your phone at all — the app works entirely offline as a private logbook.

Where it's stored

Synced data is stored with Supabase, a backend hosting provider. Dictated text, when sent, is processed by Anthropic (the company behind Claude) solely to sort it into the right sections of your debrief, and is not used to train their models beyond their standard processing terms. Speech recognition, when it can't run on your phone, is handled by Apple.

Other people's information

If you're an instructor and you enter details about a student (name, jump count, notes), you are responsible for having a proper reason and their permission to hold that information. This information is private to you and not shared beyond the app's normal sync.

Deleting your data

Go to Settings → Delete my account inside the app. This permanently deletes your account and every piece of data tied to it from our servers — not just deactivates it. If you've never signed in or synced, simply deleting the app removes everything, since nothing was ever sent anywhere.

Not a safety device

Hard Deck is a logging aid. It is not an altimeter, audible, or any kind of safety-critical equipment, and sensor readings can be wrong. Never rely on it in place of your proper safety equipment or training.

Children

Hard Deck is a skydiving logbook, and in the UK the minimum age to skydive is 16. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If you're in a country with a different minimum skydiving age, the same applies to that age instead.

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, the "last updated" date at the top of this page will change. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
